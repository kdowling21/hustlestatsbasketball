About Hustle Stats

An independent basketball analytics project by Kyle Dowling.

Kyle is a lifelong basketball savant who grew up reading and memorizing player stats and box scores in the newspaper every day. He has a degree in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance and has always had a knack for the statistical and probabilistic side of things.

He built Hustle Stats Basketball as a passion project to study what box scores don’t always pick up, such as the impact that decision-making, effort and matchups have on winning.

What’s here

Shot ProfileWhere every player and team shoots from, how well they finish, and how many points their shot choices add or cost. Hustle Plus-MinusPoints per 100 possessions added through steals, blocks, charges, rebounding and the small effort plays. MatchupsWho takes the toughest defensive assignments, how scorers fare against them, and Team Defense Impact.
How it’s built

The short version

Data: NBA.com play-by-play for every game since 2020-21, plus player tracking, shot tracking and matchup data, refreshed every Monday during the season.

Methods: every rate is compared with a baseline and pulled toward it by how little data stands behind it, so small samples can’t top a leaderboard. Lineup-adjusted models handle what box scores can’t see. The full write-up is on the Methodology page.

Independent: not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA or any team.
