Trend Watch

Weekly shot-diet shifts

Every Monday the site compares each qualified player's and team's shot diet (rim, paint, mid-range, 3PT, foul trips) this season vs last season, and over the last 4 weeks vs earlier. The biggest change relative to its noise is featured; anyone featured in the last 6 weeks is skipped. Minimums: players 150 attempts this season and 400 last season (120 in each window for the 4-week view).
